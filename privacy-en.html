Privacy Policy

Applies to: Crystonis / Indexonis / Spectronis / Pacbedonis (the "Apps")
Provider: Geekonis ("we")
Effective: 30 September 2026 (first issued 29 August 2026)

1. Summary

We do not collect personal information from users of the Apps. No account is required, no advertising is shown, and no analytics SDK is embedded. Data created or opened in the Apps stays on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other identifiers
  • Advertising IDs or unique device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contacts, call logs or SMS
  • Usage statistics or behavioural history

We operate no server that could identify a user, and the Apps send us nothing.

3. Data stored on your device

The Apps store the following only in their private storage on your device. Uninstalling an app removes it.

  • Crystal structure files (CIF) and images you open, and the results computed from them
  • Calculation parameters and instrument settings
  • Debug logs used for troubleshooting

4. Network access

Crystonis and Indexonis connect only when you run a search. Only the search string and a structure identifier are transmitted; nothing that identifies you is included. The Crystallography Open Database is a public database operated by an unrelated third party.

5. Images and text recognition (Indexonis)

Indexonis analyses photographs and images from your device and reads text within them (for example the numbers on a scale bar). This analysis and the text recognition run entirely on your device. Neither the images nor the recognition results are transmitted to us or to any third party.

6. Sharing

The Apps can hand results or debug logs to another app. This happens only when you tap share and choose a destination. Nothing is sent to a server of ours; how the receiving app handles the data is governed by that app's privacy policy.

7. Permissions

Spectronis requests no permissions at all.

Indexonis can analyse a photo you take, but it does not request the camera permission: it hands the capture over to the camera app already on your device and only receives the resulting image. For the same reason it does not request storage permission — picking an existing photo goes through your device's own image picker.

8. Crash reporting

We embed no third-party crash reporting tool. However, for apps distributed through the Google Play Store, Google may automatically collect crash information and provide it to the developer in aggregate (Android vitals). That collection is performed by Google; we do not control its content or scope. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details.

The Apps do write troubleshooting logs to your device, but those reach us only if you choose to send them using the share function.

9. Children's privacy

The Apps are professional tools for researchers and engineers working with electron microscopes and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
