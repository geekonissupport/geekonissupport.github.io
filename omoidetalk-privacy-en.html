Privacy Policy — Omoide Talk

Applies to: Omoide Talk (the "App", io.github.geekonis.omoidetalk)
Provider: Geekonis ("we")
Effective: 22 November 2026 (first issued)

This policy covers Omoide Talk only. Our other apps have their own policies.

1. Summary

The App reads a chat-history text file you exported from LINE, and the photo and video files left on an old Android phone, and puts the pictures back into their correct places in the conversation. All of that matching is done entirely on your device. Your chat history, your photos and your videos are never uploaded anywhere. We operate no server.

Two things do leave your phone, and nothing else: an advertising identifier when an ad is shown, and purchase information exchanged with Google Play. Both are described in section 2.

2. Information collected through third-party services

Google AdMob — used to show a rewarded video advertisement. Watching one ad unlocks one week of one conversation. AdMob receives an advertising identifier and ad request data in order to select and serve the ad. If you have bought the App's paid unlock, the advertising SDK is never initialised and none of this happens. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses App & Play data.

Google Play Billing — used to sell and to restore the one-time purchase. The App asks Google Play whether this Google account owns the product, and Google Play returns a purchase record. This happens at app start, when you return to the App, when you buy, and when you tap restore. Your name and email address are not part of it. See Google's privacy policy.

3. Information we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contacts, call logs or SMS
  • The text of your conversations, or any part of it
  • Your photos, your videos, or anything derived from them

We have no server that could receive any of this. The App sends us nothing at all.

4. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following only in its own storage on this phone. Uninstalling the App removes all of it.

  • The chat-history text file you selected, and the timeline built from it
  • Photo and video files copied from your old phone, and an index of them
  • Small signatures (perceptual hashes) computed from those files, used to match a video to its place in the conversation
  • Which weeks of which conversation you unlocked by watching an ad
  • Whether Google Play reports that you own the paid unlock, and when that was last checked
  • Your chosen colour theme, and which side of the conversation is you
  • Debug logs used for troubleshooting

Ad unlocks are stored on this phone only, so they are lost if you uninstall the App. The purchase is held by Google Play against your Google account, so it can be restored after reinstalling.

5. Reading your old phone over USB

When you connect an old Android phone with a USB cable and authorise the connection on that phone, the App copies photo and video files from it. This is the heart of what the App does, so it is worth being precise about the limits.

  • Read only. The App never writes to, modifies, renames or deletes anything on the old phone.
  • Five folders, fixed in the program. The list cannot be changed by you, by us, or by any server. It is:
    • /sdcard/Android/data/jp.naver.line.android/files/chats
    • /sdcard/Android/data/jp.naver.line.android/storage/toyboximg
    • /sdcard/Pictures/LINE
    • /sdcard/Pictures/LINE_MOVIE
    • /sdcard/Movies/LINE
  • Nothing bundled, nothing downloaded. The App contains no command-line tool and downloads no executable code. It speaks the Android debug protocol itself, over the USB cable only.
  • The connection is between your two phones. No part of it goes over the internet, and no part of it reaches us.

6. Folders you choose on this phone

The App can also search a folder of backups that you pick yourself. It reads the folder you grant it, through your device's own folder picker, and copies matching files into its own storage. It does not ask for the "All files access" permission, and it cannot see any folder you have not picked.

7. Writing files back to this phone

When you tap save or move on a restored picture, the App writes a copy to the location you choose through your device's own picker. That happens only when you ask for it.

8. Network access

Your conversations, photos and videos are never part of either connection. There is no third connection.

9. Purchases

The paid unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, sold through Google Play's billing system. Payment is handled by Google; we never receive or see your card number or any other payment details.

Google Play gives us, as the developer, a record of the transaction: the product, the price, the date and the country or region of purchase. It does not include your name or email address. We use it only for accounting and for handling refunds or questions about a purchase.

Because the purchase is held against your Google account, it can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling, at no further charge. The App re-checks with Google Play periodically so that a refunded purchase stops granting access.

10. Advertising preferences

Ads are served by Google AdMob and are shown only when you choose to watch one. You can review or reset your advertising identifier, and control personalised advertising, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads, or at Google's Ad Settings.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the App asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice at any time from Ad privacy settings on the App's "About this app" screen. If consent is not given, no ad is requested.

11. Permissions

The App declares the permissions below. Three are declared by us; the rest are added by the Google Play Billing and Google Mobile Ads libraries.

PermissionWhy
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC To keep matching running while the screen is off
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show the progress notification for that service. Matching still works if you decline.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, WAKE_LOCK Required by the billing and advertising libraries
com.android.vending.BILLING Required to use Google Play's billing system
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS Declared by the advertising library

The App requests no camera permission, no location permission, and no broad storage permission. In particular it does not request "All files access" (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). Choosing a chat-history file, a backup folder, or a place to save a picture all go through your device's own picker.

12. Crash reporting

We embed no third-party crash reporting tool beyond what is already part of the services in section 2. For apps distributed through the Google Play Store, Google may automatically collect crash information and provide it to the developer in aggregate (Android vitals). That collection is performed by Google; we do not control its content or scope.

13. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose tool for restoring media into an exported chat history and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
