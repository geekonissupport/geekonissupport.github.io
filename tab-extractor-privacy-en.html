Privacy Policy — TAB Extractor

Applies to: TAB Extractor (the "App")
Provider: Geekonis ("we")
Effective: 24 October 2026 (first issued)

This policy covers TAB Extractor only. It is different from the apps covered by the Geekonis privacy policy (Crystonis / Indexonis / Spectronis / Pacbedonis): unlike those apps, TAB Extractor shows advertising to free users and uses a third-party service to manage subscriptions.

1. Summary

The App does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address or similar identifiers. The videos and images you select, and everything extracted from them, are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. To run the free/Pro system and to show ads to free users, the App uses two third-party services, described in section 2.

2. Information collected through third-party services

RevenueCat (all users) — used to determine whether you have an active Pro subscription. RevenueCat assigns the App an anonymous app-user identifier and receives the device's Google Play purchase/subscription token so it can check subscription status. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Google AdMob (free users only) — used to show an interstitial ad while a video is being processed. AdMob receives an advertising identifier and ad request data to select and serve the ad. If you are a Pro subscriber, the advertising SDK is never initialised and none of this happens. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses App & Play data.

If you subscribe, Google Play also shares transaction data with us as described in section 8.

3. Information we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contacts, call logs or SMS
  • The content of your videos, images or exported files

We operate no server of our own that receives your content; the App sends us nothing.

4. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following only in its private storage on your device. Uninstalling the App removes it.

  • The video or image you select, and the panels extracted from it
  • PDF or image files you export
  • Whether you have already used your one free watermark-free export
  • Debug logs used for troubleshooting

5. Network access

ConnectionWhenWhat is sent
RevenueCat App start, and when you tap "Restore purchases" Anonymous app-user ID, Google Play purchase/subscription token
Google AdMob Free users only, while a video is processing Advertising identifier, ad request data

Your video, images and exported files are never included in either connection.

6. Processing of your video and images

Extracting panels from a video or image, and generating the PDF/image export, is done entirely on your device using a locally bundled video/image processing library. Nothing about the content of your video or images is transmitted to us or to any third party.

7. Sharing

The App can hand an exported file to another app. This happens only when you tap share and choose a destination. Nothing is sent to a server of ours; how the receiving app handles the file is governed by that app's privacy policy.

8. Purchases and subscriptions

Paid features are sold as subscriptions through Google Play's billing system, managed on our behalf by RevenueCat. Payment is handled by Google; we never receive or see your card number or other payment details.

When you subscribe, Google Play and RevenueCat give us, as the developer, a record of the transaction: the product, the price, the date and the country or region of purchase. It does not include your name or email address. We use it only for accounting and for handling refunds or questions about a purchase.

You can check or cancel a subscription in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment process.

9. Permissions

The App requests Internet and network-state access only (needed for sections 2 and 5). It does not request camera or storage permission: picking a video, image or export destination goes through your device's own file/media picker.

10. Advertising preferences

Ads shown to free users are served by Google AdMob. You can review or reset your advertising identifier, and control personalised advertising, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads, or at Google's Ad Settings.

11. Crash reporting

We embed no third-party crash reporting tool beyond what is already part of the services in section 2. For apps distributed through the Google Play Store, Google may automatically collect crash information and provide it to the developer in aggregate (Android vitals). That collection is performed by Google; we do not control its content or scope.

12. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose tool for extracting tablature/score panels from video and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
