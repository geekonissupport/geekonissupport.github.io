Geekonis

Android apps for people working at an electron microscope. They share one design goal: let you decide while still standing at the instrument.

Apps

  • Indexonis Indexes electron diffraction patterns and identifies the structure
  • Crystonis Reads a crystal structure from a CIF file and shows the atomic model
  • Spectronis Helps you choose between EDX and EELS, using simulated spectra and overlap checks
  • Pacbedonis Simulates STEM images, CBED and PACBED, and estimates specimen thickness from an experimental PACBED

All four are bilingual: the interface is English by default and switches to Japanese on a Japanese device.
